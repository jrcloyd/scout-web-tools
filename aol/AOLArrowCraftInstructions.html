🏹 Arrow of Light Career Arrow Craft Guide

Page 1: Den Leader Supply Guide & Rank Band Reference (24-Inch Wooden Arrow)

🧰 Den Leader Supply Checklist & Prep Guide

Note for Den Leaders: The Den Leader is in charge of gathering supplies before the meeting. Each arrow uses a 36-inch wooden dowel cut to 24 inches. You can either pre-cut the dowels to 24 inches ahead of time or let the Scouts measure and cut their own dowels with a handsaw under adult supervision. Each rank or camp band is 1.5 inches wide with a 1-inch space in between. If a Scout did not earn a particular rank or camp, that 1.5-inch band spot is left unpainted as blank wood.

🪵 Per Scout Materials

  • 1 Wooden Dowel (1/4" × 36", cut to 24") (Home Depot )
  • 1 Stone Arrowhead: Stone Arrowheads (Amazon)
  • 3 Feathers (Fletching): Arrow Feathers (Amazon)

🎨 Paint & Taping Supplies

  • 1-Inch Masking Tape: Using 1-inch wide tape makes spacing the 1-inch gaps between the 1.5-inch paint bands simple and consistent.
  • Acrylic Craft Paints: Silver/Gray, Purple/Lavender, Green, Light Blue, Red, Orange, Yellow, and Black.
  • Paint Brushes: Small foam brushes or craft brushes, paper plates, water cups, and paper towels.
  • Drying Holder: A cardboard box with holes poked in the top (or foam block) to hold arrows upright while drying.

🔨 Tools & Assembly

  • Ruler or Tape Measure & Pencil: For marking the 24-inch dowel length and 1.5-inch paint bands.
  • Handsaw & Fine Sandpaper: Needed if Scouts cut their own dowels to 24 inches.
  • Hot Glue Gun & Glue Sticks: For attaching the stone arrowhead and 3 feathers.
  • Optional Twine: Brown twine or embroidery floss to wrap around the arrowhead and feather ends.

🛠️ Scout Build Instructions: Making Your 24-Inch Arrow

Check or uncheck the boxes below to preview your arrow. Unchecked bands stay blank wood space on the 24-inch shaft.

1

Measure, Cut & Sand Your Dowel to 24 Inches

If your Den Leader already pre-cut your dowel to 24 inches, start at step 4 below.

  1. Lay your 36-inch wooden dowel flat on the table and use a ruler or tape measure to measure 24 inches from one end.
  2. Make a pencil mark at 24 inches. Hold the dowel firmly in a miter box or clamp on a workbench, and cut along your mark with a handsaw using steady strokes.
  3. Lightly sand both ends and the shaft with sandpaper until smooth.
  4. Write your initials lightly in pencil on the very tip of the dowel where the arrowhead will cover it so you can identify your arrow.
⚠️ Saw Safety: Keep your holding hand well away from the saw blade and only cut with an adult leader supervising.
2

Tape Off Your Bands (1.5" Paint Width & 1" Masking Tape Spacing)

Each band slot is 1.5 inches wide with a 1-inch unpainted gap between slots. Because the masking tape is 1 inch wide, wrapping one strip of tape creates the exact 1-inch space between bands.

  1. Starting from the back end (feather end) of your 24-inch dowel, measure 1.5 inches in. Wrap a piece of 1-inch masking tape just behind the 1.5-inch mark so the back edge of Slot 1 is protected.
  2. From the front edge of that tape, measure 1.5 inches down the shaft and make a small pencil mark. This 1.5-inch open wood section is Slot 1 (Arrow of Light).
  3. Starting right on your pencil mark, wrap a single strip of 1-inch masking tape around the dowel. This 1-inch tape strip forms the 1-inch gap before Slot 2.
  4. Continue down the arrow (1.5" open wood then 1" tape) for all 9 slots. After Slot 9 (Bobcat), wrap a final piece of tape 1 inch from the front tip.
  5. Press the edges of every tape strip firmly with your thumbnail so paint cannot seep underneath.
3

Paint Your Earned Bands & Leave Unchecked Bands Blank

  1. Look at your personal arrow strip or the color table on Page 1 to see which ranks and camps you earned.
  2. If you did not earn a rank or camp slot, leave that 1.5-inch slot unpainted as natural blank wood.
  3. For each band you earned, brush on two thin, even coats of the matching paint color while rotating the dowel.
  4. Place your arrow upright in the drying holder for 15–20 minutes until dry, then gently peel off all the masking tape.
4

Hot Glue Your 3 Feathers (At the End or with Overlap)

  1. Your 3 feathers attach at the back end of the arrow (near the Silver Arrow of Light band), spaced evenly around the shaft like the letter "Y" (120 degrees apart).
  2. Feathers can be glued either on the unpainted back end of the dowel or with some overlap across the first band section.
  3. Apply a thin line of hot glue along the bottom spine of the first feather and press it straight onto the shaft for 10 seconds. Rotate the dowel one-third of a turn for the second feather, and another one-third turn for the third feather.
🔥 Hot Glue Safety: Melted glue and the glue gun nozzle are very hot. Use a craft stick or pencil eraser to press down feather edges rather than bare fingers.
5

Hot Glue Your Stone Arrowhead

  1. At the front tip of the dowel (near the Black Bobcat band), apply a dab of hot glue to the end of the wood shaft (or into a shallow notch if pre-slotted by a leader).
  2. Press the stone arrowhead onto the tip so it points straight ahead and hold it steady for 20–30 seconds until the glue cools. Optional: wrap twine around the base of the arrowhead and feather ends with a drop of glue.
